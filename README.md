# HUEVOKE V8 — Scoped FORM Scroll + Lotus Hover Preview

## FORM
- Scroll is intercepted only when the mouse/cursor is over the FORM macro rotating-gallery area.
- Wheel/trackpad over the left FORM wordmark, right-side full image, header, page margins, or any area outside the FORM macro gallery scrolls the homepage normally.
- While the pointer is over the macro gallery and the FORM section is fully visible, wheel/trackpad advances the rotating gallery smoothly.
- Dragging inside the macro gallery still works.
- At the first/last FORM object, scrolling outward releases naturally back to normal page scrolling.

## Lotus Bloom
- No scroll lock.
- Uses: lb1.2, lb1.4, lb2.2, lb2.3, lb2.4.
- Hover/touch magnifies only the selected image.
- Hover/touch on lb1.2 or lb1.4 shows lb1.5 as a side preview.
- Hover/touch on lb2.2, lb2.3 or lb2.4 shows lb2.5 as a side preview.
- Side preview remains hidden at all other times.
- Touching elsewhere closes the preview.
