Studio

HUEVOKE approaches wall décor as an object in space — shaped by edge, relief, proportion and the way a room receives light.

From surface
to form.

We begin with a wall, but design in depth.

Each object is developed around controlled relief, clean geometry and restrained palettes. Premium MDF gives the work a stable sculptural base; precise fabrication builds the depth; an ultra-matte finish keeps the surface quiet so shadow becomes part of the composition.

The intention is not to make a conventional print or wall panel. It is to create an object that changes subtly with distance, angle and daylight.
