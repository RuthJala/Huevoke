Studio

HUEVOKE approaches wall décor as an object in space — something shaped by relief, edge, material and the way a room receives light.

From surface
to form.

We begin with a wall, but design in depth.

Each object is developed around controlled relief, clean geometry and a restrained palette. Premium MDF provides a stable sculptural base; precision cutting builds the form; an ultra-matte finish keeps the surface quiet and lets shadow do the work.

The result is not intended to behave like a print or conventional wall panel. It is conceived as a sculptural object that changes subtly with distance, perspective and daylight.
